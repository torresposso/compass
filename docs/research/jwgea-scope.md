# JWGEA Scope Across Chart Types

Research into where Jeffrey Wolf Green's Evolutionary Astrology (JWGEA) analysis
applies. **JWGEA core elements** (as defined for Compass):

- **PPP** — Pluto Polarity Point = Pluto longitude + 180°
- **Nodal axis** — TRUE North/South lunar nodes (not mean node)
- **Nodal rulers** — domicile rulers of the sign each node occupies, using
  MODERN rulership (Scorpio→Pluto, Aquarius→Uranus, Pisces→Neptune; rest traditional)
- **Skipped steps** — planets forming a ~90° square to the nodal axis

All findings below are Western/tropical ONLY. Vedic/sidereal approaches are out of scope.

---

## 1. Natal chart — DOES APPLY (core/intrinsic)

JWGEA is the literal foundation of Green's method. The "Planetary Method of Chart
Interpretation" is built on:

> "the location of the South Node of the Moon, and the location of its planetary
> ruler by house and sign; the meaning of the polarity point of Pluto by house and
> sign; the location of the North Node of the Moon, and the location of its
> planetary ruler by house and sign."

— Jeffrey Wolf Green, *Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul*, Vol. 1 (1985),
Ch. 10 "A Planetary Method Of Evolutionary Chart Analysis"; restated in the
School of Evolutionary Astrology course syllabus
(https://schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com/flash-course/ and
https://www.jwgaea.org/pluto).

The skipped step is defined natally:

> "anytime you have a planet square the nodes, including the moon or Pluto, in
> evolutionary terms you have what are called skipped steps … the person must
> recover those skipped steps in order to evolutionary proceed."

— J.W. Green, "Planetary Method" forum transcript,
schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com (also covered in Pluto Vol. 1, "Pluto in aspect
to the Moon's Nodes").

The PPP itself is "the degree exactly opposite natal Pluto … the evolutionary
growth direction in this lifetime." — Astrolium summary of Green
(https://astrolium.com/guides/evolutionary-astrology); consistent with Pluto Vol. 1
Ch. 1 "Sign, house & polarity point."

**Conclusion:** YES — natal always carries full JWGEA. (Already decided; confirmed.)

## 2. Progressed chart — DOES APPLY, RECOMPUTED from progressed positions

A progressed chart (secondary progression of a natal) is a valid chart read in its
own right, and Green's paradigm explicitly includes progressions as a layer of
"evolutionary development through Time":

> "The natal signature and requirements operate throughout life. The transiting
> movements of Mars, progressed planets forming aspects to natal Mars … and
> Pluto's movement through transits … and other planets forming aspects to Pluto
> through transits or progressions all correlate to the evolutionary development
> and requirements through Time in this life."

— J.W. Green, Pluto School transcript (Yumpu / SOEA course material).

The SOEA course syllabus lists "Pluto in transit, progression, solar & lunar
return" and "transits of other planets to these points, progressions, and solar
returns" as part of the EA paradigm
(https://schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com/flash-course/). Deva Green's NORWAC
lecture "Evolutionary Progressions/Development of the Soul" is devoted to this
(https://norwac.net/downloads/green-deva-evolutionary-progressions-development-of-the-soul/).

Critically, EA reads progressed planets *against the nodal axis* as skipped steps:

> "the progressed movement of a natal planet that squares the nodes (known in
> evolutionary astrology as a skipped step) can transfer the focus from one nodal
> point to the other, adding a new layer to the resolution work."

— Rose Marcus, "The Secondary Progressed Chart: Back to Basics"
(https://rosemarcus.com/progressions/).

She also confirms the baseline: "the natal chart is always the prevailing matrix or
bottom line reference point for all other chart calculations." This means the
*progressed* JWGEA is recomputed from progressed positions but is interpreted
**relative to** the natal JWGEA, which remains the soul's intrinsic signature.

Because progressed Pluto moves (slowly) and the progressed TRUE nodes move
(≈1.5° per 30 years — Astrolium), the progressed PPP, nodal axis, nodal rulers, and
skipped steps are all meaningfully different from the natal ones and are watched
for slow shifts in evolutionary emphasis.

**Conclusion:** YES — a progressed chart carries its OWN recomputed `jwgea` field,
derived from progressed Pluto (→PPP), progressed TRUE nodes (→axis + rulers), and
progressed planets (→skipped steps, including progressed planets squaring the nodal
axis). The natal `jwgea` is preserved as the root and the progressed `jwgea` is read
against it.

## 3. Transit chart — DOES NOT get its own full JWGEA; transits ACTIVATE the natal axis

A transit chart (transiting bodies over a natal) is a *timing/activation* layer, not
a standalone soul map. Green treats transits as "evolutionary transitions" — events
in which the soul's existing evolutionary work becomes acute. The relevant JWGEA
structure for transits is the **transiting lunar nodal axis**, used relationally:

> "this is going to correlate with what I call the apparent issue. If we locate the
> planetary rulers of these transiting Nodes … then wherever [that ruler] is, that's
> going to be the actual issue. So, the transiting Nodes show the apparent issue,
> while the positions of the planetary rulers of those transiting Nodes in the natal
> chart show the actual issue."

— J.W. Green, "Evolutionary Transitions" (SOEA article, 1997/2017),
https://schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com/articles/evolutionary-transitions/.

> "When you have the transiting Nodes squaring the natal Nodal axis, these are what
> I call crossroads. The crossroads can correlate with a crisis in action, or with a
> crisis in belief."

— same source.

Deva Green's transit lecture focuses on "the transits of Pluto, the transiting nodal
axis, and also the transits of the natal planetary rulers of the north and south
nodes within the natal chart" (NORWAC, op. cit.) — i.e., transits are read *back into
the natal chart*, never as a freshly computed PPP of the transiting Pluto.

Skipped steps are *triggered* by transits (a transiting planet squaring the natal
nodes re-activates the skipped-step theme) but are not "recomputed" as a new
natal-type JWGEA. See Mark Jones / Psyche Astrology: "transits can also trigger
these nodal lessons … Outer planets … cause the most disruption, but also the most
potential for growth" (https://www.psycheastro.com/posts/squares-to-the-nodes).

**Conclusion:** A pure transit chart should NOT carry its own `jwgea` computed from
transiting Pluto (a transiting PPP is not part of Green's method). Instead, expose
the **transiting TRUE nodes** and look up *their rulers in the natal chart*, and
relate transiting bodies to the **natal `jwgea`** (crossroads when transiting nodes
square the natal axis; activation of natal PPP/Pluto/nodes; skipped-step triggers).
JWGEA "applies" only as a relational overlay onto the natal axis.

## 4. Synastry chart — per-chart natal JWGEA applies; no NEW combined `jwgea`

Synastry = comparison of two birth charts; it does not generate a new chart. Green's
Vol. 2 is *Pluto: The Evolutionary Journey of the Soul, Volume 2: … in Synastry and
Composite Charts* (1997; republished 2017). The EA methodology is applied by
inserting "the core EA paradigm within one person's chart into the other's chart, to
understand how the evolutionary necessities of each Soul affect the other's Soul."

— SOEA course description, https://schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com/flash-course/.

Thus each person's **natal `jwgea`** is what matters, and the cross-contacts are read:
a partner's planet squaring your nodal axis is a recognized "skipped step" trigger in
relationship ("A partner's (or transit) Sun square your Nodes …") —
https://www.psycheastro.com/posts/squares-to-the-nodes.

**Composite chart (sibling of synastry, worth noting):** A composite is a *derived*
midpoint chart and DOES get its own EA treatment. Green Vol. 2 contains an entire
chapter "PLUTO IN THE COMPOSITE CHART" with per-house entries such as
"Composite Pluto in the 1st House or Aries / Polarity Point in the 7th House or
Libra" (sample TOC:
https://schoolofevolutionaryastrology.com/school/wp-content/uploads/essential-relationship-needs-cover-toc-sample-chapter.pdf).
So a composite chart kind carries its OWN recomputed `jwgea` (from composite Pluto →
PPP, composite TRUE nodes → axis/rulers, skipped steps from composite planets).

**Conclusion (synastry):** No new combined `jwgea` field. Each participant's natal
`jwgea` applies; compute cross-chart contacts (especially planets squaring the
other's nodes, and Pluto/PPP-to-planet contacts) as relationship features. If a
**composite** chart kind is supported, it gets its own recomputed `jwgea` exactly
like natal.

## Data-model recommendations for Compass

| Chart kind | Carry `jwgea`? | How computed |
|---|---|---|
| `natal` | **Yes (always)** | From natal Pluto → PPP; TRUE nodes → axis + modern nodal rulers; natal planets squaring nodal axis → skipped steps. |
| `progressed` | **Yes (recomputed)** | From progressed Pluto → PPP; progressed TRUE nodes → axis + rulers; progressed (and natal) planets squaring the (progressed/natal) nodal axis → skipped steps. Interpreted against the root natal `jwgea`. |
| `transit` | **No standalone `jwgea`** | Expose transiting TRUE nodes + their *natal-chart* rulers; relate transiting bodies to the natal `jwgea` (crossroads when transiting nodes square natal axis; activation/trigger of natal PPP/nodes/skipped steps). |
| `synastry` | **No new field** | Reuse both participants' natal `jwgea`; compute cross-contacts (planet squaring other's nodes, Pluto/PPP-to-planet). |
| `composite` (if supported) | **Yes (recomputed)** | From composite Pluto → PPP; composite TRUE nodes → axis + rulers; composite planets squaring composite nodal axis → skipped steps. |

### Concrete guidance

- **`jwgea` is a first-class field on `natal`, `progressed`, and `composite` chart
  objects only.** It is absent on `transit` and `synastry` objects.
- For **`progressed`**, recompute all four elements from the progressed positions
  (not "carry over" the natal values). Keep a reference to the source natal chart's
  `jwgea` for comparative reading.
- For **`transit`**, implement a `transitNodes` structure (transiting TRUE N/S nodes
  + rulers looked up in the natal chart) and a `relatesTo: natalJwgea` link, rather
  than a `jwgea` block. The "crossroads" condition = transiting nodal axis square to
  natal nodal axis.
- For **`synastry`**, compute an inter-chart aspect set; flag any contact where a
  planet from chart A squares the nodal axis of chart B (and vice versa) as a
  skipped-step trigger, and any PPP/Pluto contact between the two charts.
- **Constants for all kinds:** always use the TRUE lunar node; always use modern
  rulership (Scorpio→Pluto, Aquarius→Uranus, Pisces→Neptune) for nodal rulers; PPP =
  Pluto longitude + 180°.
